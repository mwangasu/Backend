# CivicLens AI

> **Transforming citizen voices into actionable development priorities with AI.**

## Overview

CivicLens AI is an AI-powered decision support platform that helps constituency leaders transform thousands of citizen opinions into clear, actionable development priorities.

Citizen feedback is often collected through public participation forums, suggestion boxes, SMS, WhatsApp, letters, and online forms. This information is typically scattered, duplicated, and unstructured, making it difficult and time-consuming for Members of Parliament (MPs), Members of County Assembly (MCAs), and county governments to analyze and act upon.

CivicLens AI addresses this challenge by leveraging **Google Gemma 4** to automatically understand, categorize, summarize, and prioritize citizen feedback. The platform converts unstructured public input into meaningful insights that support transparent, evidence-based planning and resource allocation.

## Problem Statement

Public participation is essential for democratic development, but analyzing thousands of citizen submissions manually is inefficient and prone to inconsistencies. Decision-makers often struggle to identify recurring issues, measure urgency, and determine which priorities require immediate attention.

## Solution

CivicLens AI uses artificial intelligence to process citizen feedback and generate actionable insights. The platform automatically:

* Categorizes community issues (Water, Roads, Healthcare, Education, Sanitation, Security, etc.)
* Detects recurring concerns and duplicate reports
* Assigns priority and urgency levels
* Generates concise constituency planning reports
* Recommends practical actions for decision-makers
* Visualizes community priorities through an interactive dashboard

## Key Features

* AI-powered citizen feedback analysis using Google Gemma 4
* Automatic issue categorization and summarization
* Interactive analytics dashboard
* Priority ranking of development needs
* AI-generated constituency reports
* Actionable recommendations for leaders
